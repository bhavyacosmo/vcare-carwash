Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

HYDROSHINE AUTO SPA - FZCO HydroShine operates car wash and auto care services in Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, UAE. This policy explains how we handle your personal information.

Information we collect

When you book a service, we collect your name, mobile number, vehicle details (such as plate number, make and model), booking details, service history and payment information.

How we use it

We use this information to manage your bookings, provide our services, process payments, and send you booking confirmations, service status updates and receipts, including via WhatsApp.

WhatsApp messages

WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy

We send service-related messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta. Your phone number and message content are shared with Meta only to deliver these messages. To stop receiving WhatsApp updates, reply STOP or contact us.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as messaging, hosting and payment providers, or when required by law.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting obligations, then delete it securely.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.
